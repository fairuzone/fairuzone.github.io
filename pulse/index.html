    How recovery is calculated

    After 3 or more check-ins, your HRV and resting HR are compared with your own rolling 30-day baseline. HRV above your normal and resting HR below it both mean better recovery. Sleep is compared with your target, and hard exercise the day before lowers the score a little. Weights: HRV 45%, sleep 35%, resting HR 20%. This is a wellness estimate, not a medical device.
